The band

Vodou is an eight-piece band that lives for the stage, based in Lehighton, PA. We play rock, funk, soul, and whatever else the night calls for. Rooted in the spirit of the ’70s but open to almost every genre, we play for the love of it and for the people in the room.

How we got here
  • 2023 — We began as Quantum Trebuchet, a small experimental group with guitar (Jed), bass (Zane), drums (Kian), and a different vocalist. New guitarists came in as Jed had to step out. We then played two gigs at CCTI's student talent show. We then split due to school and work.
  • 2025 — Jed, Zane, and Kian reformed, and went through the names Blue Voodoo and Voodoo before landing on Vodou. We added keys (Aiden), current vocals (Rowan), and Horns. We played Weisstock, CCTI's open house and an acoustic open gig. We began finding our live sound through covers.
  • 2026 — We returned to Weisstock for a second year and played Musikfest after being accepted via the ArtsQuest Industry Conference. We started writing originals (still in progress) and plan to get an album out soon!

Promoter Bio

Vodou is an 8-piece high-energy rock, funk, and soul band based in Lehighton, PA. Featuring a full horn section, tight rhythm section, and dynamic lead vocals, Vodou delivers hard-hitting live performances spanning 70s rock classics, funk grooves, and original material.
